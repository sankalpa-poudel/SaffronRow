Frequently asked questions

How can I place an order?

Choose your size and add items to your bag. Enter your name and delivery address, then send the order directly through WhatsApp.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across Nepal. Delivery charges and estimated arrival time are confirmed when you place your order.

What payment methods are available?

Cash on delivery is available for eligible locations. Bank transfer and digital payment options can also be arranged through WhatsApp.

How do exchanges work?

Contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order details. Items must be unused and returned with their original tags.
